# Decision Log

- **2026-10-13** — Stack: C + SDL3 + OpenGL, chosen by Danny.
- **2026-10-13** — No prebuilt game engine (Godot/Unity/etc.); small libraries OK, keep it lean.
- **2026-10-13** — Linux first, targeting Omarchy (Arch, Wayland/Hyprland).
- **2026-10-13** — Visual direction: neon vector style (Tempest-like) with bloom.
- **2026-10-13** — Assets: only Danny's or clearly licensed, credited. Nothing ripped from the original.
- **2026-10-13** — Title: **Starpost Returns**. Visual nods to the C64 original are in scope, amped up with Tempest neon flair. No ripped art or audio.
- **2026-10-13** — Fire is an instant beam. A tracer shows the current lane. Firing makes the beam rapidly widen and brighten like a pulse laser. The exact pulse shape can still be iterated.
- **2026-10-13** — Movement is one lane at a time (left/right rotation), like the original and Tempest. No direct 8-way lane select.
- **2026-10-13** — The original 8-hits-per-wave rule is replaced by a persistent shield. A walker that reaches the base drains the shield. Each cleared wave restores a tunable amount of shield (not a full reset). Empty shield is game over.
- **2026-10-13** — Superzap stays in M4 with the flyer. It is not in the core loop and it is not held for a later power-up phase.
- **2026-10-13** — M2 wave tuning is one table in `src/game/game.c`: count `5 + 3i` (cap 48), speed `0.15 + 0.025i` (cap 0.70), spawn interval `0.90 - 0.04i` (minimum 0.22), `i = wave - 1`. Later waves are longer, faster, and denser.
- **2026-10-13** — Score is 100 per kill and 500 on wave clear. Arrivals do not score. Shield recharge is applied on clear and capped at max. Empty shield skips the clear. The clear screen holds for 1.5 s, then the next wave starts.
- **2026-10-13** — The HUD (wave, score, shield bar) and the clear screen use a small stroke vector font in `src/render/render.c`. No font files.
- **2026-10-13** — M3 neon: scene in an RGBA16F target, bright-pass plus a 5-tap separable Gaussian at quarter resolution, added back and tonemapped. F2 toggles bloom. Line fragments and screen shake live in `src/fx`, spawned from a renderer frame diff, not from game-logic fields. The HUD does not shake.
- **2026-10-13** — M4 flyer: spirals in from the rim at 0.72× walker speed and 1.8 rad/s. Lane 0 is up, clockwise as the index increases. A beam hits it only inside 0.55 of a lane sector around the tracer. Base contact drains 3 shield. It scores 300. Flyers start on wave 2, `1 + (wave - 2) / 2`, cap 4.
- **2026-10-13** — M4 superzap: Z or Shift. One charge granted per wave, unused charges carry, cap 4. A press with at least one enemy on screen spends a charge, kills them, and flashes. Zero charges does nothing. The HUD shows the stock.
- **2026-10-13** — M5 audio: a built-in synth, no samples. The simulation emits `GameSnd` events; SDL only plays them. M mutes. `-` and `=` change gain. A living flyer holds a warble that releases when it is gone.
- **2026-10-13** — M5 gamepad: hot-plug. D-pad and left stick step one lane (deadzone 0.35, then the keyboard repeat). No 8-way lane pick. South/A and RT fire. East/B and LT superzap. Start restarts after game over. Keyboard stays active.
- **2026-10-13** — Held fire, keyboard and gamepad, waits `STARPOST_FIRE_COOLDOWN` between shots. Danny asked to slow it: 0.16 s became 0.32 s.
- **2026-10-13** — The top HUD bar counts down the wave. Shield stays on the pips around the base. `starpost_wave_gauge` is the share of the quota still unresolved. A kill, a base arrival, and a superzap kill each drain it, including flyers. It is empty when the wave clears and full on the next wave.
- **2026-10-13** — M7 energy, Danny's numbers: the cap is 100. Clearing a wave grants 20, clamped to the cap. Superzap costs 20 and no longer grants a charge each wave. Energize (Q / left shoulder) costs 5 and lasts 3 s; it kills walkers already in the aimed lane, anything that spawns or sits in that lane, and flyers whose angle is inside that lane window. Slow (E / right shoulder) costs 10, lasts 5 s, and halves walker speed only. Flyers keep full speed. A short deny sound and red flash play when energy is short.
- **2026-10-13** — M7 playtest: starting energy is 0, not 20. Spread (X / West) costs 2 energy per shot, not 1.
- **2026-10-13** — Energy-economy playtest: a run starts at 20 again. A normal shot, including held fire, costs 1. A destroyed walker returns 1 and a destroyed flyer returns 5, from the beam, spread, energize, and superzap. A cleared wave grants 10, not 20. Cap stays 100. `free_shot_at_zero` is 0, so 0 energy denies the shot with the buzz and a red tracer. Set it to 1 to fire that shot for free. Softlock: at 0 energy, with the flag off, shots are refused while walkers are still arriving, so the player can only take hits until the wave ends or the shield breaks.
- **2026-10-13** — M7 controls: Superzap is Y (North) and the left trigger. Energize is B (East). The left shoulder is unbound. Keyboard stays Z/Shift, Q, X, and E. Initials gain an END column. Confirm on a letter moves to the next column. The score is saved only when END is confirmed. `STARPOST_INITIALS_DELAY` (1 s) ignores entry input, and a button held through that delay does not count until it is released and pressed again.
- **2026-10-13** — Shield no longer recharges when a wave clears, on every difficulty (`shield_recharge` is 0). R or the left shoulder buys one pip for `repair_cost` (10) energy, up to the max. A press is one pip. Short energy or a full shield plays the deny buzz and flash and does not spend. Repair works mid-wave and on the wave-clear screen, and not after game over.
- **2026-10-13** — Menu confirm and fire share Space, J, and gamepad A. Fire is level-triggered, so the held confirm was a shot on the first play tick. Entering play (title start, pause resume) ignores fire until that button is released. A new press then shoots.
- **2026-10-14** — Normal fire and spread launch projectiles at `STARPOST_SHOT_SPEED` 2.5 (the rim is 0.4 s away). Each shot kills the first walker, splitter, child, or in-window flyer it meets, using the meeting time inside the step so a fast move cannot tunnel. A shot at the rim is removed. Held fire waits 0.16 s again (`STARPOST_FIRE_COOLDOWN`), down from 0.32 s. A flyer killed by one of these projectiles scores and pays 3× (900 points and 15 energy). Energize and superzap still pay 300 and 5. The shot's energy cost is spent when it is fired.
- **2026-10-14** — Energize can be live on more than one lane. Each press spends 5 and sets that lane's own timer to 3 s. Pressing it again on a lane that is already live replaces the remaining time with a fresh 3 s and still costs 5. It does not add 3 s on top of what was left. Every live lane uses the same kill rule and the same glow.
- **2026-10-14** — Walker speed is about 15% slower so the extra enemies stay readable. Wave 1 is `0.15 * 0.85` (0.1275), and each later wave adds `0.025 * 0.85` (0.02125). The speed cap stays 0.70. Splitter, child, and flyer speeds are still multiples of the walker speed, so they slow by the same amount.
- **2026-10-13** — Splitter: a lane enemy at 0.62× walker speed. A kill spawns two smaller units in the neighboring lanes, wrapping, at the parent's distance. Those units move at 1.25× walker speed, so the split is faster than a normal walker. The parent is worth 200 points and 1 energy and drains 2 shield. Each small unit is worth 50 and 1 energy and drains 1. They are drawn as lime hexagons, the children smaller. A superzap kills a splitter and does not spawn the children. Energize destroys a child that lands in the energized lane. The same spread that breaks a splitter does not erase the new units. Reaching the base does not split. Slow covers splitters and the children. Advanced schedules them from wave 3 (`1 + (wave - 3) / 3`, cap 3). Beginner starts at wave 5, Hyper at wave 2. The gauge counts each splitter as three from the start, so a split cannot raise it, and a zap or a base hit resolves the two children that never appear.
- **2026-10-13** — M6 meta: the title menu is Start / Difficulty / High Scores / Quit. Difficulty is a tune on the wave table (Beginner ×0.75 speed, −2 walkers, ×1.35 interval, flyers from wave 4, recharge 3; Advanced is the base table; Hyper ×1.35 speed, +3 walkers, ×0.75 interval, flyers from wave 1, recharge 1). Esc or P or Start pauses during play and no longer quits. Game over opens 3-letter initials when the score makes that difficulty's top 10, then returns to the title. Scores are `STARPOST-SCORES 1` under `$XDG_DATA_HOME/starpost-returns/` (or `~/.local/share`), written by rename over a temp file. A missing or corrupt file loads as an empty table.
- **2026-10-13** — An 8BitDo SF30 Pro left the game hung on the poll thread. SDL's Switch HIDAPI driver (and the 8BitDo and Xbox HIDAPI drivers) issue output reports from `SDL_PollEvent`. Linux `hidraw` `write()` does not time out in userspace, and the SF30 Pro does not answer the Switch USB high-speed command. With enhanced reports left on, a quiet wired pad is sent `ForceUSB` every poll, and a quiet Bluetooth pad is closed and reopened through that setup. Starpost turns those HIDAPI drivers off and reads the kernel gamepad node, which SDL already maps. A stick that jitters around the deadzone stays latched until it returns inside 0.20. A frame cannot drain more than 64 pad events or run more than 8 sim steps.
